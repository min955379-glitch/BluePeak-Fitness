Four zones. One purpose: better training.

Every zone is laid out so you can move from heavy lifting to sprints to recovery without waiting on equipment.

Zone A

Power Lifting Platforms

Calibrated Eleiko and Rogue power racks, bumper plates and platforms for squat, bench and deadlift sessions.

06 — FAQ

Questions before you walk in

Do I need lifting experience to join? +
No. Strength Foundations is built for beginners and covers the squat, hinge, press and pull with a coach watching your form. Experienced lifters can start in any class.
What is included in the 3-day pass? +
Full floor access to the lifting platforms, sled turf and erg bay, a complimentary InBody composition scan, and one group session during the three days.
Is there a joining fee? +
Initiation is $0 through Sunday for new members. Membership prices are listed in the Memberships section above.
Can I cancel my membership? +
Membership terms, including notice periods and cancellation steps, are explained at sign-up. Ask the front desk for a copy before you commit.
Is there parking? +
Yes. Member parking is free on site at 742 Mercer Industrial Way.
What are the opening hours? +
The club is open from 05:00 to 23:00. Coached classes follow the timetable above, and the live status in the top bar shows whether we are open right now.
